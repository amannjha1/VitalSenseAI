# VitalSense AI
### Detect Early. Care Better.

VitalSense AI is an AI-powered healthcare platform designed to support preliminary health-risk assessment, preventive care, patient monitoring, and personalized health insights.

## Overview

VitalSense AI explores how Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning can improve healthcare accessibility and help people better understand health-related information through intelligent insights and user-friendly technology.

## Key Features

- **AI-Assisted Symptom Assessment:** Provides informational guidance based on user-reported symptoms.
- **Health Risk Analysis:** Offers preliminary health insights based on user-provided information.
- **Interactive Health Dashboard:** Displays vital signs, health indicators, and trends.
- **Preventive Healthcare:** Provides general wellness suggestions and preventive-care recommendations.
- **Medical Report Analysis:** Supports medical report uploads and structured information extraction where implemented.
- **Personalized Health Insights:** Presents understandable results and possible next steps.
- **Responsive Design:** Provides an accessible experience across desktop and mobile devices.

## Responsible AI and Healthcare Safety

- VitalSense AI is intended for informational and educational purposes.
- It does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
- AI-generated health insights must not be treated as confirmed medical diagnoses.
- Risk assessments must not be described as clinically validated without appropriate validation.
- Users should consult qualified healthcare professionals regarding medical concerns.
- Potentially urgent symptoms require prompt professional medical attention.
- Demo environments should use synthetic data rather than real patient records.
